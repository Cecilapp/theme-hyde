Hyde ships with eight optional color schemes, based on the [base16 default colors](https://github.com/chriskempson/base16), plus a Cecil one.

<!-- excerpt -->

Set the `hyde.theme` option in your `config.yml` to apply one of them:

```yaml
hyde:
  theme: blue # red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, magenta, brown or cecil
```

The color scheme changes the background of the sidebar and the color of links.
